# Pitch: offline Android cashier ledger for small food shops

(Pricing is a proposal, not a market quote. No testimonials exist yet.)

## 30-second script
"Kuya/Ate, do you still count sales on paper or a notebook? We have a simple Android app that records every sale, cash and GCash, with no internet needed. At closing it tells you how much cash should be in the drawer, you count, and it shows any difference. Voids are logged so nothing disappears, and you can back up your data to your phone. Can I show you in 2 minutes on your own phone?"

## 30-second script, Taglish (suggestion)
"Kuya/Ate, nagbibilang pa po ba kayo ng benta sa papel o notebook? Meron kaming simpleng Android app na nagre-record ng bawat benta, cash at GCash, kahit walang internet. Pag closing, sasabihin niya kung magkano dapat ang cash sa drawer, bibilangin ninyo, at kita agad kung may sobra o kulang. Naka-log ang lahat ng void, kaya walang nawawala, at may backup pa sa phone ninyo. Pwede ko po bang ipakita sa inyo, 2 minuto lang, sa sarili ninyong phone?"

## One-page offer
- Works fully offline (load shedding, weak signal, no problem).
- Tracks cash vs GCash per sale and per day.
- Day-close cash count: expected vs counted, shortage/overage shown.
- Void tracking: who/when/what, so voids are visible.
- Backup/export of the ledger.
- Runs on an ordinary Android phone; no extra hardware.
- Free 7-day trial on the owner's phone, set up with their menu.
- Honest limits: it is a ledger, not a payment processor; it does not connect to GCash, it records what you enter.

## Suggested pricing (proposal)
- Setup (menu entry + 15-min training): P500 one-time, or free for first shops.
- Option A: P299/month per shop.
- Option B: P1,999 one-time license, 1 year of updates.
- Pilot: first 5 shops get 1 month free in exchange for feedback.

## Objections
- "I use a notebook, it's fine." Keep it; trial both for one week and compare the day-close difference.
- "No internet/phone is old." It works offline; ask which Android version and test it first.
- "Staff won't learn it." Few buttons; train in 15 minutes; menu pre-loaded.
- "Too expensive." Compare P299/month to one missed or miscounted sale per week; offer the one-time option.
- "What if I lose my phone?" Backup export; restore on a new phone.
- "Is my data safe?" Data stays on the device unless you export it; no account needed.
- "GCash is tracked in the GCash app already." Yes, but this puts cash and GCash in one daily total per shop.
